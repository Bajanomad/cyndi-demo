About Cyndi

Thirty seven years in, still the one they call

Cyndi Williams has been selling real estate for more than 37 years. She ran her own commercial brokerage in California's East Bay, owned a hotel, a restaurant and half of a real estate company in Cabo, and spent a decade as Broker Owner of Cabo Premiere before joining Selling Sur Real Estate Co.

She is the Cerritos expert: her personal book is centred on Cerritos, Pescadero and Todos Santos, the Pacific side she has called home since 2007. Owners from the US and Canada trust her with their Baja homes because she treats every listing like it is her own.

Her rule is simple: “Don't dream it, do it. Call me to make it happen.”

The life comes first

People do not buy square feet down here. They buy mornings on a swimmable beach at Cerritos, coffee as the fishing boats go out at Todos Santos, desert light over the Sierra de la Laguna, and a town where everybody knows their name by the second visit.

That is the order on this page too: the place first, the life it gives you second, and the property details after, where they belong.
